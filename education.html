Quantum Computing — Library Notes

Single-qubit foundations built one script at a time, with every circuit drawn and every result checked against the algebra. Gates as deterministic unitaries, measurement as a separate probabilistic projection, and the basis as a choice that decides what you can see — ending with how those three ideas assemble into BB84.

State vectors · X and Hadamard · Ry(θ) and the Born rule · the invisible Z phase · X-basis measurement · S, T and the third Pauli basis · the four BB84 states · Bloch sphere geometry
