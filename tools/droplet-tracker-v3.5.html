Smart builds a per-pixel median background from N sampled frames (rejects static bright spots), then locates the droplet by weighted centroid on the background-subtracted ROI. Frame-difference uses |current − previous| instead of a median model (lighter, no prebuild). Centroid Frac is the fraction of the peak used as threshold. Rotate spins every frame about its center before tracking (positive = clockwise, negatives allowed) so 1D motion can be aligned to the x-axis; the frame keeps its original size, so corners are cropped. Tracked coordinates and the exported CSV are in this rotated frame.
