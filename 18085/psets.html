Collaboration and AI

You are encouraged to discuss the problems with each other. All final write-ups must be your own, in your own words. State clearly in your submission who you worked with and what outside resources — including AI tools — you consulted.

The line

Using an AI system in place of your own reasoning, without saying so, is a violation of the collaboration policy. Using one as a study aid and saying so is not.

Quizzes and the final

Two quizzes, 50% of the grade together, held in class during the regular lecture period. Quiz 1 — Monday, October 19, covering Lectures 1–12. Quiz 2 — Monday, November 16, covering Lectures 13–18. Each takes the whole period, in the usual room.

  • Quizzes: one letter-size sheet of handwritten notes, one side.
  • Final exam (35%): Wednesday, December 16, 9:00 am–12:00 pm, room 6-120. One sheet, both sides.

Solutions to old problem sets and quizzes circulate freely. Every set this term keeps the architecture you would recognise but changes the numbers, so working an old solution backwards will not help you.
