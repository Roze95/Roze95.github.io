How to submit

  • Gradescope, as a single PDF, by 11:59 pm ET on the due date. Match each problem to its pages when you upload.
  • Attach all code you used — as an appendix in the same PDF, plus the notebook or .m file itself if the problem asks for it.
  • Python or MATLAB, your choice. Reference solutions are posted in both. Neither is worth more credit than the other.
  • Show the reasoning, not only the answer. A correct number with no derivation earns little; a clear derivation with an arithmetic slip earns most of the points.

Collaboration and AI

You are encouraged to discuss the problems with each other. All final write-ups must be your own, in your own words. State clearly in your submission who you worked with and what outside resources — including AI tools — you consulted.

The line

Using an AI system in place of your own reasoning, without saying so, is a violation of the collaboration policy. Using one as a study aid and saying so is not.

Quizzes and the final

Two quizzes, 15% each, held in class during the regular lecture period. Quiz 1 — Monday, October 19, covering Lectures 1–11. Quiz 2 — Monday, November 16, covering Lectures 12–17. Each takes the whole period, in the usual room.

  • Quizzes: one letter-size sheet of handwritten notes, one side.
  • Final exam (30%): registrar-scheduled, Dec 14–18. One sheet, both sides.

Solutions to old problem sets and quizzes circulate freely. Every set this term keeps the architecture you would recognise but changes the numbers, so working an old solution backwards will not help you.
