Online software

Browser-based tools developed in the lab of Prof. John Bush (MIT Department of Mathematics). Each tool runs entirely in your browser — no installation, no upload of your video to any server.

Droplet Tracker — Standalone (v3)

Interactive tracker for walking-droplet experiments. Load a video, define the region of interest, and track the droplet trajectory frame-by-frame; export results as CSV for downstream analysis in MATLAB or Python.

Launch tracker →

Works best in a recent Chrome, Firefox, or Safari. Your video stays on your machine.

Couder Walker Frequency Lab — Noise Edition

A browser-based signal generator and mixing console for driving the Faraday-bath speaker rig used in walking-droplet experiments. Five independent tone lanes (each with its own frequency, amplitude, and phase) feed a master bus alongside a band-limited noise generator whose center frequency, bandwidth, and amplitude can be adjusted in real time. A built-in scope shows the noise channel and the combined signal + noise waveform side by side, making it easy to prepare controlled stochastic forcing for Couder-style bouncing- and walking-droplet experiments.

Launch frequency lab →

Routes audio out via the Web Audio API — use the browser’s output device selection to send the signal to your shaker amplifier.
