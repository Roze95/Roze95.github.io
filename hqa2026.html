About the meeting

HQA 2026 brings the hydrodynamic-quantum-analogs community to MIT for two days of talks on walking droplets, pilot-wave hydrodynamics, and the classical wave systems that reproduce quantum-like behavior.

The meeting spans experiment, simulation, and theory: bouncing and walking droplets on a vibrating bath, wave-mediated collective dynamics, stochastic and non-Markovian pilot-wave models, analogs of Aharonov–Bohm and multi-slit interference, and foundational questions about what such classical systems can and cannot say about quantum mechanics. Talks run from 9am to early evening on both days, with a conference dinner at PAGU on the Monday evening.

When & where. Monday–Tuesday, August 10–11, 2026 · Massachusetts Institute of Technology, Cambridge, MA.
All lectures will be in 2-449. Lunches will be in 2-290.

Topics

Themes running through the two-day program:

Pilot-wave hydrodynamics

Walking and bouncing droplets on a Faraday bath — dynamics beyond resonance, drop–bath impacts, tunable global forcing, motion on noisy surfaces, and pilot-wave dynamics in three dimensions.

Collective dynamics & active matter

Wave-particle active matter, ensembles of walking droplets, collective pilot-wave dynamics in experiment and simulation, and the oscillatory Cheerios effect between bouncing drops.

Stochastic & non-Markovian dynamics

Stochastic pilot-wave models, the influence of path memory on walker transport, non-Markovian branched flow, and Lorenz-like reductions of the walker system.

Analogs of quantum interference

Hydrodynamic realizations of the Aharonov–Bohm effect, the Kennard phase, triple-slit diffraction, and single-particle diffraction within pilot-wave models.

Topology, localization & transport

Topological transport and emergent localization in hydrodynamic analogs, wave quantization in periodic media, and routes toward general-relativity analogs.

Foundations & realist approaches

Stochastic electrodynamics as a realist account of quantum phenomena, what Bell’s theorem really means, and static and Lorenz-model Bell tests with walkers.

Mathematical frameworks

A Lorentz-covariant Lagrangian framework for pilot-wave dynamics, quantum graphs, quantum mechanics on Riemannian manifolds, and the orbital dynamics and energetics of wave-propelled particles.

Waves beyond the droplet

Strong nonlinear resonances of water waves in confined domains, acoustic pilot-wave dynamics, and hydrodynamic analogs applied to optical beam transport.
